# Team Roles

Your team has 10 members:

- 6 AI members
- 2 Data Science members
- 2 Cybersecurity members

## AI Members

### AI 1: Red Team Agent

Builds attacker decision logic.

Deliverables:
- Red Team action selection
- Attack path reasoning
- Red Team explanation text

### AI 2: Blue Team Agent

Builds defender decision logic.

Deliverables:
- Response action selection
- Containment recommendations
- Defender explanation text

### AI 3: Incident Report Generator

Builds natural-language reporting.

Deliverables:
- Executive summary
- Timeline summary
- Recommended actions

### AI 4: Alert Explanation Assistant

Explains why alerts are dangerous.

Deliverables:
- Alert explanation function
- MITRE technique explanation
- Simple chatbot panel if time allows

### AI 5: Scenario Intelligence

Improves scenarios and difficulty levels.

Deliverables:
- More scenarios
- Easy/medium/hard modes
- What-if defense simulation

### AI 6: Dashboard and Integration

Owns the Streamlit/frontend experience.

Deliverables:
- Streamlit dashboard
- Browser demo polishing
- Final demo script

## Data Science Members

### DS 1: Detection Models

Owns anomaly detection and classification.

Deliverables:
- Isolation Forest experiment
- Random Forest experiment
- Accuracy/precision/recall/F1

### DS 2: Risk Scoring and Analytics

Owns scoring and evaluation.

Deliverables:
- Risk scoring formula
- Before/after defense comparison
- Charts and results table

## Cybersecurity Members

### Cyber 1: Red Team and MITRE Mapping

Owns attack realism.

Deliverables:
- Attack scenario design
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Safe simulated attack steps

### Cyber 2: Blue Team Rules and Playbooks

Owns defense realism.

Deliverables:
- Detection rules
- Alert severity definitions
- Incident response playbooks
